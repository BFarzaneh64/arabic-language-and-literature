# Arabic Language and Literature

[![DOI](https://zenodo.org/badge/DOI/10.5281/zenodo.22040769.svg)](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22040769)

Collected scholarly articles on Arabic language and literature.

## Volume 1

**Arabic Language and Literature: Scholarly Articles, Volume 1**

**Author:** Babak Farzaneh  
**Language:** Persian  
**Publication type:** Collected scholarly encyclopedia articles  
**Zenodo DOI:** https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22040769

This volume brings together a selection of scholarly encyclopedia articles on Arabic language and literature, originally published in different volumes of *The Great Islamic Encyclopaedia*. The articles are written in Persian and cover topics in Arabic linguistics, grammar, rhetoric, literary history, and related fields.
